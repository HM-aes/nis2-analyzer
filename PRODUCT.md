# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users
Compliance consultants who run NIS2 gap analyses for client companies (mostly Dutch IT firms). They create clients, upload the client's policy and security documents, trigger the AI analysis, review the resulting gaps, and deliver a report. The public landing page speaks to the client companies themselves, but the dashboard is the primary surface.

## Product Purpose
Turn a company's own documents into a list of NIS2 compliance gaps, each tied to a specific NIS2 article, so a consultant can review and deliver findings without weeks of manual reading.

## Positioning
Article-cited gaps, fast: hours instead of weeks of consultant time, with every gap traceable to the directive.

## Design Principles
- Every finding is traceable: show the NIS2 article and the source it came from.
- The consultant's workflow comes first: each screen has one clear next action.
- Status is always visible: audit and gap state should be scannable in any list.
