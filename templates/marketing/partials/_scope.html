01 — Am I in scope?

Most organisations don't know which side of the line they're on.

NIS2 sorts in-scope organisations into two tiers. They carry different supervision — and different consequences. The assessment places you precisely; here's the distinction it's built on.

proactive supervision Essential entity

Higher scrutiny, before anything goes wrong

Large operators in high-criticality sectors — energy, transport, banking, health, water, digital infrastructure, public administration.

  • Regular audits, on-site inspections and security scans — not incident-triggered
  • Fines up to €10M or 2% of global annual turnover, whichever is higher
  • Management can be held personally accountable under Article 20
reactive supervision Important entity

Lighter touch — until you're on the radar

Medium-sized operators, and organisations in the broader set of important sectors — postal, waste, chemicals, food, manufacturing, research, digital providers.

  • Supervision triggered by incidents or evidence of non-compliance
  • Fines up to €7M or 1.4% of global annual turnover, whichever is higher
  • Same core Article 21 security obligations still apply
